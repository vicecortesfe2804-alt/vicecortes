Vice Cortes Studio

Vice Cortes Studio is the internal publishing tool of Vice Cortes, a channel that posts short clips of GTA 6 live streams from streamers who have authorized clipping. Every clip credits the original streamer with their @ and a link to the full live stream.

What the tool does

  • Lets the channel owner sign in with the channel's own TikTok account (Login Kit).
  • Uploads finished vertical clips to that account's TikTok inbox as drafts (Content Posting API). The owner reviews and publishes each one in the TikTok app.
  • It is used only by the channel owner, for the channel's own account. It does not post on behalf of other people.
